- Palette size: 64 colors

- Broadcast date: 2026-07-25 20:25:00 (Asia/Shanghai, UTC+08:00)
- Broadcast sampling timestamp: 37:05
- Sampling method: manual screenshot color sampling; extraction and
  deduplication of colors observed in a downloaded official published work
- Redistribution authorization: none identified

The 32 observed colors combine and deduplicate colors from the two official
source types above. Screenshot, image encoding, and export differences can
produce small RGB variations, so these values are observations rather than a
claim of exact official specifications. The other 32 colors were selected by
the project author to fill gaps in the basic RGB range. They are predictions
and must not be represented as officially published or confirmed colors.
