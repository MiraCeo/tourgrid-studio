# Third-Party and Excluded Materials

This file identifies materials distributed with Tourgrid Studio that are not
licensed under the project's Apache License 2.0.

## Palette data

Affected paths:

- `palettes/`
- `frontend/js/natural-64-v2.js`
- archived palette copies under `palettes/archive/` and `frontend/js/archive/`

The current palette contains colors observed in a downloaded official
published work, colors sampled from official promotional broadcast footage,
and provisional colors predicted by the project author. Observed colors are
not represented as exact official specifications. Future palette revisions
may replace provisional colors with colors sampled from official sources.

The palette data is excluded from Apache-2.0. Tourgrid Studio does not grant
permission to reproduce or redistribute official game-related or event-related
materials. All applicable rights remain with their respective rights holders.

Provenance details are recorded in
[`docs/legal/palette-provenance.md`](docs/legal/palette-provenance.md).

## Dependencies and base images

Python dependencies, container base images, and other third-party components
downloaded while building or running Tourgrid Studio remain subject to their
own license terms. They are referenced by project configuration but are not
relicensed under Apache-2.0.
